# Harnessing AI and Computation for Sustainable Materials, (Bio)Chemistry, and Energy
The Yoon Group advances AI and multiscale modeling to accelerate chemical and materials discovery for energy and sustainability. Our research drives the development of environmentally sustainable chemicals, materials, and processes for a carbon-neutral future.
