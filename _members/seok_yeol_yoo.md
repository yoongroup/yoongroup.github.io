Dr. Seok Yeol Yoo joined the Yoon Group at KAIST in August 2026 as a prestigious InnoCORE Postdoc Fellow! Seok Yeol obtained his PhD in Chemistry from KAIST, working with Dr. Yoonsu Park and Dr. Mu-Hyun Baik in 2025. Most recently, Seok Yeol has worked as a postdoc at IBS before joining the Yoon Group in August 2026.
At Yoon Group, Seok Yeol is working on sustainable and energy materials for C1 catalysis.
